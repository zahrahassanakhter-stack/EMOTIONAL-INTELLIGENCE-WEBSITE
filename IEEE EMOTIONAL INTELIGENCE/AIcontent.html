Understanding Emotional Awareness

Emotional awareness is the ability to recognize what you are feeling in the moment and understand why you are feeling it. Instead of reacting instantly, it helps you pause, reflect, and respond wisely.

Key Insights

  • Every emotion has a trigger — identify it.
  • Your reaction is a choice, not a habit.
  • Naming your emotion reduces its intensity.

Try This

Next time you feel stressed, pause and ask yourself: “What exactly am I feeling right now?”
